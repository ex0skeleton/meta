Meta Wiz user manual

A guide to what the image results mean.

Getting started

Drop an image or choose one from your computer. The preview and basic metadata appear first. A deeper scan may finish later. Use the tabs to inspect its origin, search every detected tag, edit common fields or clean a copy.

Content origin

Content Credentials (C2PA) are a record embedded in some images. They can describe who or what created or edited an image. Declared generator is the named tool or organization, Software agent is a specific app or model, and Digital source describes the type of source, such as AI-generated media. These are declarations, not an independent AI detector.

An OpenAI or GPT name in the results is useful evidence to investigate, but a plain metadata tag or visible text can be altered. A signed credential is stronger only when its validation and signer trust are checked.

Credential status, in plain language

Manifest read successfully
A C2PA record was opened, and this scan did not report a validation problem. It does not mean its signer was trusted. Meta Wiz does not check signer trust here.
Validation issues found
The record reported a validation issue. Treat its origin claims with caution; inspect the raw C2PA output for details.
Detected · verification unavailable
A C2PA-like marker was found, but the record could not be parsed or checked. No conclusion about authenticity is possible from this status.
Not detected
No Content Credentials were found. The image could still be AI-generated or could have had its credentials removed.

Other labels you will see

AI declaration
Embedded data says the content is AI-generated or names an AI tool. It is a claim, not a detection score.
Recorded actions
Changes listed by a credential, such as creating, cropping or editing. “No actions declared” only means none were found here.
GPS / EXIF
GPS can reveal a location. EXIF can include camera settings, dates and device information.
Metadata fields
The number of readable tags found by the scan. It is not a measure of privacy or authenticity.

All Metadata and Edit

Search a tag or value in All Metadata, or export the detected data as JSON. In Edit, enter the common fields you want in a separate image copy. JPEG writes EXIF; PNG, WebP and TIFF use compatible metadata fields. Enter both latitude and longitude together. Editing a signed image may make its existing credential invalid, and this app cannot create a genuine signed credential.

Clean and verify

Remove GPS only removes the JPEG EXIF location directory. Remove EXIF block removes JPEG EXIF but may leave XMP or Content Credentials. Complete pixel clean rebuilds the visible image and discards most original embedded metadata, but may change quality, color or file format. Compare the processed copy before downloading. Text or clues visible in the picture itself are not removed.

Privacy and limits

Your selected image is processed in this browser and is not uploaded by Meta Wiz. The page loads fonts and processing libraries from external providers when connected to the internet. Some formats or engines may not work in every browser. A missing claim never proves where an image came from.
